# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, sexual identity and orientation, or technology choices.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- demonstrating empathy and respect;
- giving and accepting constructive technical feedback;
- focusing criticism on code, evidence, and outcomes;
- acknowledging mistakes and correcting them;
- prioritizing the health of the community and project.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, sexualized content, deliberate intimidation, publishing private information, sustained disruption, and conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Responsibilities and enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing acceptable behavior. They may edit, remove, or reject comments, commits, issues, pull requests, and other contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer through the contact options on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed as confidentially as practical. Retaliation against a reporter is prohibited.

## Scope

This code applies in repository spaces and when an individual publicly represents the project or community.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers may apply correction, warning, temporary restriction, or permanent exclusion based on impact, context, repetition, and risk to participants.
